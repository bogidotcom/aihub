# Introducing AIHub Kyrgyzstan — Building Digital Businesses with AI

We're excited to announce the launch of **AIHub Kyrgyzstan**, a new community hub dedicated to bringing artificial intelligence and digital entrepreneurship to the heart of Central Asia.

## Why Kyrgyzstan?

Kyrgyzstan is at a pivotal moment. The country has a young, tech-savvy population hungry for innovation and new opportunities. Yet access to cutting-edge AI knowledge and tools has been limited. We saw this gap and decided to build something that could change that.

The vision is simple: **empower Kyrgyzstan's brightest minds to build the digital businesses of tomorrow using AI**. Not by importing Silicon Valley, but by building something rooted in our community, our values, and our unique perspective.

## What is AIHub?

AIHub is a space where:

- **You learn vibe-coding** — a new approach to building with AI at the core. It's not about learning syntax; it's about building AI-native businesses and products where AI does the heavy lifting.
- **You meet builders** — Attend events, hackathons, and workshops. Connect with other entrepreneurs, developers, and innovators in Kyrgyzstan.
- **You build products** — Access consulting, custom development, and AI integration services to ship your ideas fast.
- **You scale together** — From first concept to shipped product. We're here to support the entire journey.

Think of it as a convergence point for AI learners, digital entrepreneurs, and technologists in Kyrgyzstan who want to create something meaningful.

## Who's Behind This?

AIHub is founded by experienced builders who've been in the crypto, Web3, and AI spaces for years. We've shipped products across multiple continents, raised capital, and led teams. Now we're channeling that expertise into building something for Kyrgyzstan.

Our CEO has led over 20 people across 10+ shipped products, from DEX aggregators to decentralized VPNs to AI compute networks. He's bringing that same founder energy and execution mindset to AIHub.

## What's Coming

We're launching with three core offerings:

1. **Vibe-Coding School** — Learn to build with AI. Choose between a one-month intro, a three-month residency with real client projects, or 1-on-1 mentorship.
2. **Events & Community** — Meetups, workshops, hackathons, and community dinners. Stay tuned for details.
3. **Development Services** — Need help building? Custom development, AI integration, consulting, and audits. We'll help you ship faster.

## Stay Tuned

Our **first event is coming soon**. We'll gather the Kyrgyzstan tech community together to celebrate the launch, share knowledge, and start building. Whether you're a developer, entrepreneur, student, or just curious about AI — there will be something for you.
